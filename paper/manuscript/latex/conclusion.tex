\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
RG-MTM formulates topology, geometry, temporal alignment and reference guidance within one position--topology framework. A common interval representation, four-term objective and renderer support topology--time and topology--geometry regimes inspired by earlier work, followed by reference-preserving and topology-relaxed regimes. Independent weights express preferences, while topology and position budgets impose measurable limits.

Positional certificates and optimal barriers explain which compromises are attainable and support inverse threshold selection. New operating-regime experiments and budget-dependent maps distinguish exact model properties, candidate-set solutions, raster measurements and similarity to native baselines. Retained temporal, volumetric and static studies show both useful reference alignment and limits of projection and topology relaxation. The contribution is a system for expressing, diagnosing and controlling these competing design requirements, with explicit evidence for the behavior of each constructed map.
